Hanzo’s Den

A Japanese-inspired vintage retreat, made over a weekend with a friend, to set the mood of a cinematic scene.

Year

2023

With

Neena Fatima

Output

Still renders and a short animation

Tools
  • Blender
  • DaVinci Resolve

The main focus was mood. The biggest challenge was the lighting.

Process

  1. Idea

    Favorite anime and dream homes, one weekend.

  2. Assets

    The room and its objects, built with a friend.

  3. Lighting

    Light that sets a cinematic mood.

  4. Render

    Cycles in Blender 3.5.

  5. Grade

    Compiled and graded in DaVinci Resolve Studio.

Final. The room from above, with afternoon sun through the curtains.

Clay. The same view in plain grey, before materials.

Render passes

One frame, taken apart.

Final. Sun from the window lands on the figure and the wall behind it.

Color. Each surface’s own color, before any light. Metal has no diffuse color, so the bronze reads black.

Light. Light and shadow alone, with every surface white.

Highlights. Only the light caught by edges: the lips of the wall planks and the floorboards.

Normals. The way each surface faces, written as color.

Position. Where each point sits in the room, written as color.

Mono. A black-and-white take on the same frame, keeping warmth in the light.
