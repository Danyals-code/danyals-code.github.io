Thor’s Mjölnir

Mjölnir from the Marvel films, modeled in Blender and textured in Substance Painter to read as a real object.

Year

2022

Type

Personal study in realism

Output

Stills on two backgrounds, and animation

Tools
  • Blender
  • Substance Painter

A long focal length keeps the composition flat, and a shallow depth of field keeps the hammer in charge, quieting the background without losing its small details.
