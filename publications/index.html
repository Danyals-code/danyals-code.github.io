  • Conference paperKSDS 2026

    Assessment of Multi-View Consistency in Automated Product Form Analysis

    Danyal Sarfraz, KwanMyung Kim

    KSDS 2026 International Conference · June 2026

    Case study
    A 23-parameter pipeline turns product images into form descriptors using a silhouette mask and monocular depth. Across seven products rendered on a turntable, parameters from 16 views matched those from 180 views for 20 of 23 parameters (90% consistency or better), so sparse imagery is enough for this class of analysis.

  • Full paperIASDR 2025

    A Component-Based Approach for Developing Actions in Non-Humanoid Elderly Care Robots

    Danyal Sarfraz, Ulugbek Vahobjon Ugli Ismatullaev, KwanMyung Kim

    IASDR 2025: Design Next · Taipei, Taiwan · December 2025

    doi:10.21606/iasdr.2025.1139 Case study
    A hybrid method for designing expressive motion for Lemmy, a two-wheeled companion robot for older adults. A designer workshop produced an action catalogue, and expert interviews defined four motion components: speed, path, posture and turn type. Paired comparisons with 25 older adults set preferred values. In a validation study with 15 new participants, actions assembled from preferred components rated highest (M = 7.67 of 10), which suggests validated components can be recombined into new actions without re-testing each one.

  • PictorialIASDR 2025

    Synthetic Realities: Evaluating Human Ability to Distinguish AI-Generated Videos from Real Footage

    Danyal Sarfraz, Raja Mubashar Karim, KwanMyung Kim

    IASDR 2025: Design Next · Taipei, Taiwan · December 2025

    doi:10.21606/iasdr.2025.1050 Case study
    A controlled online study compared text-to-video, image-to-video and outpainting against real footage across film, historical and environmental themes. 87 untrained participants made 3,132 real-or-AI judgments and were correct 60% of the time. Image-to-video was the easiest to spot and text-to-video the hardest. Theme, technique and their interaction all mattered, and viewers showed a bias toward calling real footage synthetic.

  • Conference paperKSDS 2025

    Developing a Hierarchically Structured Action Catalogue for an Elderly Care Robot through Participatory Design

    Danyal Sarfraz, Ulugbek Ismatullaev, KwanMyung Kim

    KSDS 2025 International Conference · Kookmin University, Seoul · June 2025 · pp. 166–167

    Case study
    A participatory workshop with six industrial-design researchers, working in pairs from everyday user stories, produced more than 40 action ideas for Lemmy. The teams clustered them in three different ways, and the ideas were organized into a hierarchy by how often teams proposed them. All three teams favored social and emotional presence over pure efficiency, with actions like pausing near the user until acknowledged.
