About

Danyal Sarfraz

HCI researcher and designer with an MSc in Engineering Design from UNIST, South Korea. My research designs and evaluates mixed-reality, AR and AI systems with the people who use them, including an award-winning Meta Quest 3 heritage application, a life-size AR application for iPhone and Apple Vision Pro, and studies of human perception of AI-generated video.

My background is in industrial design (BSc, NUST, Pakistan) and four years of professional 3D work for international clients. I favor low-burden study methods and research tools that transfer into design practice.

Danyal Sarfraz is an HCI researcher and designer working on mixed reality, spatial interfaces and human-centered AI. Danyal holds an MSc in Engineering Design from UNIST (2026). Work at UNIST includes a Meta Quest 3 heritage app that won first place at the 3rd Korea Heritage Digital Contents Contest, a life-size AR app for iPhone and Apple Vision Pro, papers at IASDR and KSDS, a Blender add-on with more than 7,000 downloads, and work on Lemmy, a companion robot recognized with a CES 2025 Innovation Award. Danyal also holds a BSc in Industrial Design from NUST.
