About

Hi, I’m Danyal.

I’m an HCI researcher and designer with an MSc in Engineering Design from UNIST in South Korea. I design and test mixed-reality, AR and AI experiences with the people who will use them: a Meta Quest 3 app that won a national heritage contest, a life-size AR app for iPhone and Apple Vision Pro, and studies of how people judge AI-generated video.

Before that, I studied industrial design at NUST in Pakistan and worked as a freelance 3D artist for clients around the world. I’m drawn to the edge between people and technology: what we can test before anything is built, what we trust, and how we read the machines around us. I care about methods that respect participants’ time, and about tools other designers actually use.

Short bio

Danyal Sarfraz is an HCI researcher and designer working on mixed reality, spatial interfaces and human-centered AI. Danyal holds an MSc in Engineering Design from UNIST (2026). Work at UNIST includes a Meta Quest 3 heritage app that won first place at the 3rd Korea Heritage Digital Contents Contest, a life-size AR app for iPhone and Apple Vision Pro, papers at IASDR and KSDS, a Blender add-on with more than 7,000 downloads, and work on Lemmy, a companion robot recognized with a CES 2025 Innovation Award. Danyal also holds a BSc in Industrial Design from NUST.
