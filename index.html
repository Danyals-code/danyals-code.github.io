HCI researcher with an MSc in Engineering Design from UNIST. My work examines how people experience systems that bridge the digital and the physical, from mixed-reality access to cultural heritage and life-size AR evaluation of unbuilt products to human perception of AI-generated media. The findings are translated into methods and tools for design practice.

First place3rd Korea Heritage Digital Contents Contest, 2024

Bringing the Bangudae petroglyphs indoors through mixed reality

Visitors to Ulsan’s Daegokcheon Stream can view the Bangudae petroglyphs only from across the water. Field capture of the site and a Meta Quest 3 application bring its artifacts into any room at full scale.

Spatial computing · iOS and visionOS

Life-size AR as a substitute for physical prototypes

A native ARKit and RealityKit application that places a life-size robot concept in participants’ homes on iPhone and Apple Vision Pro. Participants rated control clarity 8.3 of 10, and AR 7.8 of 10 for judging scale, orientation and path.

Human-centered AI · IASDR 2025

Human detection of AI-generated video

87 participants classified 36 real and AI-generated clips. Overall accuracy was 60%; text-to-video was the hardest to detect.

Research areas

Spatial computing

Mixed reality, AR and visionOS as evaluation environments, from explorable heritage sites to products that have not yet been built.

Human-centered AI

Human perception of generative media, and AI pipelines that convert product imagery into design data.

Human–robot interaction

Legible, comfortable motion for non-humanoid robots, designed with the older adults who would use them.
