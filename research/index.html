Research

Human-centered research in spatial and intelligent systems

My research examines how people experience spatial and intelligent systems, combining mixed-reality and AR prototypes, controlled perception studies and participatory workshops. It was conducted primarily at UNIST’s Integration and Innovation Design Lab (IID Lab) under Prof. KwanMyung Kim.

Get the highlights.

  • MSc, UNIST, February 2026. Graduating in Engineering Design, two years after joining the IID Lab.

  • Thesis defended, December 2025. A framework for designing robots’ physical actions. A paper based on it is in review at ACM CHI 2027.

  • IASDR 2025, Taipei. A full paper on robot actions, a pictorial on AI-generated video, and Don Norman at the poster session.

  • Lemmy on show, 2025. Demos at CES 2025, where it won an Innovation Award, and at the Gwangju Design Biennale.

  • First place, 2024. Bangudae XR brings Ulsan’s prehistoric petroglyphs indoors on Meta Quest 3.
